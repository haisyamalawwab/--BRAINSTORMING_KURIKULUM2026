## D. RUBRIK UAS — Startup Demo Day (30% — Mg16)

| Aspek (Bobot) | Skor 4 (85-100) | Skor 3 (70-84) | Skor 2 (55-69) | Skor 1 (0-54) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Deck Storytelling (30%)** | 10 slides: Cover one-liner impactful, Problem ($ + pain), Solution demo GIF, Market TAM/SAM/SOM bottom-up valid (BPS source), Product screenshots, Traction (landing visits+conversion+MVP UAT), Business Model (pricing $), Go-to-Market (channel), Team founder-market fit, Financials & Ask ($X for Y months runway, use of funds pie) — storytelling hero's journey, design clean (max 30 words/slide), no typo | 10 slides tetapi 1 lemah (Market top-down saja tanpa bottom-up), design crowded | 8-9 slides, 2 missing (Team, Financials) atau typo banyak | <8 slides atau storytelling tidak ada |
| **MVP Fungsional (30%)** | Live demo 5m sukses: 3 langkah (onboard→core→result) klik real tanpa lag, handle edge (error toast), no crash, data persist, show AARRR dashboard live, UAT quote 1 ditampilkan | Live tetapi 1 minor lag (2s) atau 1 edge tidak handle | Live tetapi 2 bugs (need refresh), demo 50% video | Demo gagal total (video saja tanpa live) → maksimal C |
| **AARRR/CAC/LTV (20%)** | CAC/LTV di-slide dengan angka (CAC $2, LTV $15, LTV/CAC 7.5 healthy), cohort D7 35%, payback 2m, runway 15m, insight next (scale referral), Q&A jawab moat (network effect) | CAC/LTV ada tetapi 1 salah (churn missing), insight generik | CAC/LTV 50% benar, tanpa cohort | Tidak ada CAC/LTV atau salah total |
| **Delivery & Q&A (20%)** | 5m pitch tepat (4:30-5:30), voice 140wpm, eye contact, body open, handle Q&A (market valid? CAC payback? moat? competitor?) dengan data + jujur "will check", no filler "umm", time management tepat | 5m (4-6m), handle Q&A 70% dengan data, 1 "umm" banyak | Overtime >6m atau <4m, Q&A banyak tidak terjawab, filler banyak | Pitch tidak terstruktur, tidak mampu jawab Q&A dasar |
