## Bagian 1: Ringkasan Eksekutif

Program Studi **Sistem dan Teknologi Informasi (STI)** adalah program studi **hybrid** yang menggabungkan dua domain utama menurut APTIKOM:
- **Information Systems (IS)**
- **Information Technology (IT)**

Program ini dirancang untuk menghasilkan lulusan yang mampu merancang arsitektur sistem terintegrasi dan mendorong transformasi digital di tingkat enterprise.

### Mata Kuliah Pilihan Konsentrasi

**Konsentrasi 1: Enterprise Systems & Business Analytics**
- Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP)
- Business Intelligence
- Data Mining & Machine Learning
- Process Mining
- Sistem Pendukung Keputusan
- Customer Relationship Management (CRM)
- Supply Chain Management System
- Analytics for Digital Business

**Konsentrasi 2: IT Infrastructure & Cybersecurity**
- Network Security
- Ethical Hacking & Penetration Testing
- Cloud Security & Architecture
- IT Service Management (ITIL)
- IoT Security, Disaster Recovery, Wireless Security, Cybersecurity Governance

**Konsentrasi 3: Enterprise Architecture & Digital Transformation (Unggulan)**
- Enterprise Architecture (TOGAF)
- Digital Transformation Strategy
- System Integration & Middleware
- IT Risk Management
- Business Process Management
- Digital Platform & Ecosystem
- Change Management & Digital Leadership
- Smart City / Digital Government
